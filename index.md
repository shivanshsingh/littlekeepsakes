# Privacy Policy — Little Keepsakes

_Last updated: 13 August 2026_

Little Keepsakes is designed to collect nothing about you. This policy says so
plainly, and describes the one narrow exception that applies only if you choose
to buy a membership.

## The short version

**Little Keepsakes does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** The
game runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no logins, no
analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. We cannot see what you play, when you
play, or who you are.

## What stays on your device

Your progress — restored worlds, stars, your daily streak, best move counts, and
your sound and haptic settings — is saved **only on your device**, using the
system's standard local storage. It is never sent anywhere. If you delete the
app, this information is gone; we never had a copy.

## Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder, the app schedules a local notification on
your device to let you know a new keepsake has arrived. This is handled entirely
by iOS on your device. No notification is sent from a server, and enabling it
transmits nothing to us. You can turn it off any time in iOS Settings.

## Purchases

Little Keepsakes offers an optional membership and a one-time purchase. If you
choose to buy:

- The transaction is processed by **Apple** through the App Store using your
  Apple ID. We never see or receive your name, email, or payment details.
- We use **RevenueCat** to manage subscription status (to know whether your
  membership is active). RevenueCat processes the purchase receipt Apple
  provides and an anonymous, randomly generated identifier. It is used solely to
  deliver what you paid for — it is not used for advertising or tracking, and it
  is not linked to your real identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at
  https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy for details on their handling.

If you never make a purchase, none of the above applies and no data leaves your
device at all.

## What we never do

- We do not use advertising or ad networks.
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

## Children

Little Keepsakes is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children. It contains no ads and no external links to unsafe content.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
date. Because the app collects no data, we do not have a way to notify you
directly — please check this page.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: **support.littlekeepsakes@gmail.com**
