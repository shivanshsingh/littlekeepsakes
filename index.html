Common questions

  • How do I restore my membership? Open the app, tap the menu (top-left) → Store → “Restore purchases”.
  • How do I cancel? Manage or cancel any time in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. You keep the two free scenes after cancelling.
  • The scene sound isn’t playing? Check the app isn’t muted in Settings, and that your device volume is up. The soundscape plays alongside your own music.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Little Keepsakes is designed to collect nothing about you. This policy says so plainly, and describes the one narrow exception that applies only if you choose to buy a membership.

The short version

Little Keepsakes does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. We cannot see what you play, when you play, or who you are.

What stays on your device

Your progress — restored scenes, stars, your daily streak, best move counts, and your sound and haptic settings — is saved only on your device, using the system’s standard local storage. It is never sent anywhere. If you delete the app, this information is gone; we never had a copy.

Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. This is handled entirely by iOS on your device. No notification is sent from a server, and enabling it transmits nothing to us. You can turn it off any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Little Keepsakes offers an optional membership (a monthly or yearly subscription). If you choose to subscribe:

  • The transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. We never see or receive your name, email, or payment details.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status (to know whether your membership is active). RevenueCat processes the purchase receipt Apple provides and an anonymous, randomly generated identifier. It is used solely to deliver what you paid for — not for advertising or tracking, and not linked to your real identity. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

If you never subscribe, none of the above applies and no data leaves your device at all.

What we never do

  • We do not use advertising or ad networks.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

Children

Little Keepsakes is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads and no external links to unsafe content.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Because the app collects no data, we cannot notify you directly — please check this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support.littlekeepsakes@gmail.com

This policy reflects the app as built: no data collection, no network calls except Apple/RevenueCat purchase handling if you choose to subscribe. If a future version adds any data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships.
